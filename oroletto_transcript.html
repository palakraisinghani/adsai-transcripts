Turn 4

hey give me a 100 word description for the Project.
Claude's response
⎿  13 skills available
OthelloRL: teaching a machine to play Othello OthelloRL explores how far different kinds of reinforcement learning can take an agent at Othello. The agents start from nothing but the rules. On a 6×6 board, it compares classical methods (tabular learning, DQN) trained against a hand-written bot with self-play methods (DQN, PPO, AlphaZero), plus a league and co-evolution of populations of agents. Controlled experiments show why a fixed opponent stalls learning and why search-guided self-play wins. The strongest agent, AlphaZero-based Oroletto, beats depth-4 minimax about three times in four. It was then scaled to the full 8×8 board, tested against the Edax engine, and shipped as a playable web game with difficulty levels.
